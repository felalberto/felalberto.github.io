## Context

NYC's CS4All Blueprint gives educators public resources for planning and teaching computer science. This work focused on making physical computing and digital storytelling projects easier for teachers to plan, scaffold, and bring into classrooms.

## My Role

**Public Resource Contributor**

I created and adapted instructional materials for the Blueprint, with an emphasis on usable planning supports, differentiated resources, and student-facing tools.

## What We Built

- Scaffolded planning templates
- Story planners
- Differentiated instructional materials
- Student coding supports
- Facilitator-facing resources

## What Learners Did

Teachers used the materials to structure physical computing and storytelling projects, giving students clearer ways to plan ideas, code, revise, and explain their work.

## Artifacts

[Explore the CS4All Blueprint Resources](https://blueprint.cs4all.nyc/resources)

## Outcome

The published materials became part of the public CS4All resource ecosystem and have supported more than 1,000 NYC teachers implementing physical computing and storytelling work.
